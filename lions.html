Case Study

Rakaia Lions Club

A website design and build for a local Lions Club.

Freelance Project
My Roles
  • UX Design
  • Prototyping
  • Development
Brief

The Challenge

Design and develop a website for the Rakaia Lions Club that enables customers to easily browse, select, and order flower bulbs online.

Problem

The Issue

The Rakaia Lions Club currently manages bulb orders through phone calls, which has become difficult to coordinate. As order volumes have grown, the process has become time-consuming and difficult to organise, leading to a greater risk of errors, missed information, and inefficiencies.

Outcome

The Result

A website design that provides customers with a simple way to browse available flower bulbs, view product information, and place orders online. By centralising the ordering process into a single digital platform, the solution reduces administrative workload, improves organisation and accuracy, and creates a more efficient experience for both customers and the club.

Process

How I Got There

01

Research

I began by looking through field reviews of existing flower bulb websites and analysing their current ordering processes to understand how they approach online sales. This helped me consider how the ordering experience could look for the Rakaia Lions Club.

02

Prototyping

I created a hi-fi prototype for both mobile and desktop versions to allow the client to visualise the experience and sign off on the design before development.

Key Takeaway

Simplifying the ordering process can make the experience much more intuitive for customers

03

Development

I created a high-fidelity prototype using the existing branding, alongside the updated site map, ready to be handed off to the development team.

Impact

17% increase in sales in 2025 compared to 2024. Sold out within 2 months.
