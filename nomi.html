Brief

The Challenge

An open brief: identify a real problem and design a solution from scratch. I chose to focus on the loneliness and lack of belonging students face when they move somewhere new for university.

Problem

The Issue

Female students entering tertiary education often experience a lack of confidence and belonging as they transition into a new environment. Making friends is hard enough, but finding people they genuinely connect with, can be harder still.

Outcome

The Result

An app that helps university students understand what they’re like as a friend. By exploring their personality and what they value in friendships, they learn what kind of people they click with. Nomi then connects them with others they’re more likely to be compatible with, so they can meet the right people instead of leaving it to chance.
